# Production credits

This page tracks who cast, produced, or organized a tournament or match on your organization's behalf — it's shown on the relevant tournament or match page. Don't confuse it with your public member list; see [Members](/dashboard/members) for that.

Each credit card shows:

- the person
- what it applies to: an entire tournament, or a specific match
- their role: Caster, Analyst, Host, Observer, Organizer, Producer, Editor, Photographer, Translator, Referee, or a free-text "Other"
- an optional display title that replaces the role when shown publicly (e.g. "Guest analyst")

Adding, editing, or removing a credit requires the **Manage staff** permission.
