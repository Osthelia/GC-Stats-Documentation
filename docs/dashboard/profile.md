# Organization profile

The **Profile** page controls how your organization appears across GC Stats.

## General information

- Name and slug (the identifier used in your organization's URLs)
- Primary and secondary country
- Social links: X/Twitter, Twitch, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Discord
- Tags (toggleable badges shown on your public profile)

Requires the **Edit organization profile** permission (`organization.profile.edit`).

## Logos

A separate panel lets you add, replace, or remove your organization's logos:

- logo for light theme
- logo for dark theme
- neutral logo

Upload a square image, 10 MB or smaller — square images work best. Requires the **Upload/manage logos** permission (`organization.logo.upload`).

## If you can't edit

If you don't have the required permissions, the page stays visible but switches to read-only: you'll see a notice explaining why, and a simplified summary of the profile instead of the edit form.
