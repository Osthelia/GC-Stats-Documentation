# Author space

The Author space (`/dashboard/author`) is independent of any organization. You can access it if:

- your account has the individual **author** permission, or
- you write news articles for an organization (writing there requires a byline, even without the dedicated author permission).

## Sections

- **My articles** — the same table/editor as an organization's [News](/dashboard/news) page, but limited to articles under your own byline. Articles you write here skip the review workflow and go straight from Draft to Published.
- **Author profile** — your name, slug, bio, social links, and logo: the public byline shown on your articles.
